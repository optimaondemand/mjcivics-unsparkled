In this module you will go back to the roots of the American republic. You will ask what ideas the Founders took from Athens, Rome, and Jerusalem and from English charters, how the Enlightenment and the quarrel with Britain led to the Declaration, and how the Constitution was written and won approval. These questions come first so that you can see how your answers change by the end.

By the end of this module you will be able to:

  • explain what the Founders took from Athens, Rome, the Judeo-Christian tradition, and the English documents from Magna Carta to Paine;
  • compare Hobbes, Locke, Montesquieu, and Rousseau, and explain natural rights, the social contract, and consent of the governed;
  • describe the British policies that led to the Declaration and match its grievances to the rights it says were violated;
  • explain the failures of the Articles of Confederation, how the Constitution answered each one, and why the Federalists and Anti-Federalists argued over a bill of rights.
