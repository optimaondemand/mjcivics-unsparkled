A Republic Tested

How this module works. Work through the items in order, top to bottom. Read each lesson and primary source, then take the quiz that follows it. Most sets of items close with a discussion. Begin with the Intro and finish with the Outro.

By the end of this module you will be able to: explain why the Weimar Republic was weak, and how Hitler used the republic’s own rules to end it; describe how the Nazi government took away German Jews’ citizenship and carried out the Holocaust; describe the attacks of September 11 and the steps the nation took in response; explain how safety and liberty can pull against each other, using Korematsu v. United States as an earlier test.
