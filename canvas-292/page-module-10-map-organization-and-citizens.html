MODULE MAP

Organization and Citizens

How this module works. Work through the items in order, top to bottom. Read each lesson and primary source, then take the quiz that follows it. Most sets of items close with a discussion. Begin with the Intro and finish with the Outro.

By the end of this module you will be able to: describe the powers of Congress, the President, and the courts, and explain why the Founders chose three branches; explain checks and balances, judicial review, and the four ways the Constitution limits government; tell delegated, reserved, and concurrent powers apart, and compare federal, unitary, and confederal systems; explain how the Constitution is amended, how Florida’s constitution compares, and which level of government provides which services; tell the duties a citizen owes from the responsibilities a citizen chooses, explain the rights in the Bill of Rights and how the 14th Amendment carried them to the states, and describe how landmark cases and amendments widened the republic.
