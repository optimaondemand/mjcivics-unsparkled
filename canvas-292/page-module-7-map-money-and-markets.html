MODULE MAP

Money and Markets

How this module works. Work through the items in order, top to bottom. Read each lesson and primary source, then take the quiz that follows it. Most sets of items close with a discussion. Begin with the Intro and finish with the Outro.

By the end of this module you will be able to: explain what credit is and how Hamilton’s plan and the national bank built the new nation’s trust; describe the kinds of taxes, why the whiskey tax led to rebellion, and how Washington answered; explain how a market economy works, what a mixed economy is, and why a republic regulates monopolies; compare market and command economies, and use GDP per capita as evidence while naming what it leaves out.
