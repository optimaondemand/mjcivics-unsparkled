Choose one grievance and make a claim about whether it really violated a natural right such as life, liberty, or property. Support your claim with at least two facts from the lessons. Then say whether you think this grievance, by itself, was serious enough to justify independence, remembering Locke’s warning that people should not change governments for light and passing causes.
