MODULE MAP

Policy and Practice

How this module works. Work through the items in order, top to bottom. Read each lesson and primary source, then take the quiz that follows it. Most sets of items close with a discussion. Begin with the Intro and finish with the Outro.

By the end of this module you will be able to: follow the steps of an election, explain the Electoral College, and tell how the election of 1800 led to the 12th Amendment; explain Madison’s idea of faction and Tocqueville’s view of associations, judge a source for bias, and act on a local problem; describe how America has argued about its place in the world, from Washington’s warning to the Truman Doctrine; compare market, mixed, and command economies, and use GDP per capita as evidence; review the whole course by tracing each part of the Constitution to the failure it answers.
