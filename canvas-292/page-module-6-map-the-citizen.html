MODULE MAP

The Citizen

How this module works. Work through the items in order, top to bottom. Read each lesson and primary source, then take the quiz that follows it. Most sets of items close with a discussion. Begin with the Intro and finish with the Outro.

By the end of this module you will be able to: explain how Americans have answered the question of who counts as a citizen, and what citizens owe; describe how amendments widened the vote and how a vote is counted and an election is decided; explain how juries, a free press, and informed citizens check the power of government; describe how power is shared between the nation and the states, and how a citizen can act on a local problem.
