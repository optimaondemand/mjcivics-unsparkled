Origins

How this module works. Work through the items in order, top to bottom. Read each lesson and primary source, then take the quiz that follows it. Most sets of items close with a discussion. Begin with the Intro and finish with the Outro.

By the end of this module you will be able to: explain what the Founders took from Athens, Rome, the Judeo-Christian tradition, and the English documents from Magna Carta to Paine; compare Hobbes, Locke, Montesquieu, and Rousseau, and explain natural rights, the social contract, and consent of the governed; describe the British policies that led to the Declaration and match its grievances to the rights it says were violated; explain the failures of the Articles of Confederation, how the Constitution answered each one, and why the Federalists and Anti-Federalists argued over a bill of rights.
